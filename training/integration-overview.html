The Ecosystem at a Glance

How the four systems work together

STRIPE

Online Donation Payments

How a donor's credit card payment reaches your fundraiser

What Stripe handles

All credit card processing — we never touch card numbers. Stripe also manages recurring (monthly/yearly) donations automatically.

What the app handles

Records the donation, matches the donor to a Person by email, and updates the fundraiser total in real time.

ZEFFY

Importing Zeffy Donations

Two ways Zeffy data gets into the app

QUICKBOOKS

Syncing Donations to QuickBooks

Only manual donations sync — Stripe & Zeffy go to QB via bank deposit

Why this split?

Zeffy and Stripe pay out to your bank account — QB sees those as bank deposits automatically. Syncing them again from the app would double-count the money.

Manual donations

Cash and check donations don't go through a payment processor, so the app syncs those to QB as Sales Receipts. One at a time or all at once with "Sync All to QB."

Donor Matching & Approvals

How the app links donations to people in your database

Quick Reference

Which system does what

JCRB Platform

People, partners, relationships, events, fundraisers, seating charts, donor history. The central hub — everything else connects here.

Zeffy

Free fundraising platform. Donations collected there are imported here automatically via sync or real-time webhook. Email lists can be exported back to Zeffy.

Stripe

Processes online credit card donations on your public donation pages. Handles one-time and recurring gifts. We never see card numbers — Stripe does.

QuickBooks

Your accounting system. Zeffy and Stripe reach QB automatically via bank deposit. The app only syncs manual donations (cash/check) to QB as Sales Receipts.
